HOW IT WORKS

From raw feedback to insight

  1. Load sample data or upload a CSV.
  2. Clean empty reviews and invalid ratings.
  3. Classify sentiment and calculate metrics.
  4. Explore charts, filter rows, and export results.
LIMITATIONS

Simple, transparent analysis

The app uses a small English word dictionary. It may misread sarcasm, context, slang, and complex negation. Results should be checked before business decisions.
